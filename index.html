Our Story

About Pizza Lab

Where passion meets flavour

Born out of a love for real, honest food, Pizza Lab started with a simple dream — to bring gourmet-quality pizzas to everyday people at prices that make sense. We're not a chain. We're a kitchen with a soul.

Located at Godown Stop, Nachane, every item on our menu is crafted fresh, made to order, using quality ingredients. From our hand-stretched dough to our blend cheese and imported toppings, we put care into every bite.

As the owner likes to say — "Kindly keep visiting. Or else we both will starve."
